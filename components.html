Single-purpose pieces, each documented one axis at a time — label, variant, size, icon, loading, disabled — rather than as one wall of every combination. Every case carries the markup that made it, and that markup is read back out of the rendered example rather than typed beside it, so it cannot drift from what you are looking at.
