Color

Three tiers. The palettes are raw; the semantic layer maps a role to a palette step; components only ever read the semantic layer. Two observations worth keeping: the UI greys are blue-leaning (the cool ramp), and borders are always alpha-white — never a solid grey step. Swapping either one is the fastest way to lose the look.

Alpha ladders Confirmed

Every surface, border, hover and scrim in the system is built from these two ladders rather than from opaque greys. That is what lets panels stack over generated media without muddying it.

Typography

Four families and a fluid scale that steps twice — at 768px and 1280px. Size and line-height share one index, so a step change moves both together.

The tracking rule

Tracking is a function of size, not of style. This held across all 188 text tokens:

≤ 14px0%UI, labels, captions
16–20px-1%body, small headings
28–64px-2%display headings
any, caps-4%always with uppercase

One exception: menu and nav labels carry +0.1px (letter-spacing-loose), opening them slightly for scanability.

Spacing & sizing

A 4px base with 2px granularity at the small end. The names are numeric, not t-shirt sizes, which is why the scale can carry both 6px and 10px without awkward naming — and those two odd steps do real work: 6px is the standard icon-to-label gap, 10px is the tab's vertical padding.

Measured control heights Confirmed

Radius

Radius does not scale with the element. It plateaus: controls climb 6 → 8 → 10 → 12 and stop, while containers jump to 16 and 24. A 48px button and a 640px modal are only two steps apart. Counted across the live page, 12px was the single most used radius in the product.

Border & divider

Five widths, and the choice between them is meaningful: 1px for structure, 1.5px for focus and weight (control borders, focus rings), 2px reserved on every button so outline variants never shift layout.

Elevation

The most distinctive part of the system, and the easiest to get wrong. Higgsfield does not use a drop-shadow ladder. Every raised surface carries a 2px inset top sheen — light catching the upper edge — plus a soft ambient shadow. Remove the sheen and the surfaces go flat no matter how much shadow you add.

Icons

The specification is confirmed from the product's SVGs. The glyphs on this page are original drawings to that spec — Higgsfield's own icon set and its logomark are not reproduced here.

Confirmed — spec

Drawing rules

  • 24 × 24 viewBoxalways, whatever the render size
  • 1.5px strokeoutline only; fill reserved for status glyphs
  • currentColornever a hardcoded fill or stroke
  • .25 grid offsetspaths land on 6.25 / 17.75, not 6 / 18 — the half-stroke sits on the pixel
  • round capson organic paths; square on geometric ones

Optical weight is held by the stroke, not the size: a 16px icon and a 24px icon use the same 1.5px stroke, so small icons read slightly heavier. That is intentional and matches the product.

Motion

21 durations and 10 easings ship — far more than a typical system, because the product animates state rather than decorating it. The pattern that matters: enter and exit are asymmetric. Popovers enter over 180ms on an expo-out and leave in 130ms on an ease-in. Hover on a filled control is not a colour change at all but a brightness() filter.

Layout & z-index

Button

One base component, five properties. Three structural decisions are carried over exactly and are worth knowing before you touch it:

  • display: inline-grid with grid-auto-flow: column — not flex. A lone label centres optically without line-height correction.
  • A 2px transparent border is always reserved, so switching to the outline variant never moves anything.
  • Press feedback on filled variants is filter: brightness() — 0.8 on hover, 0.6 on press — so a variant needs one colour token, not three.

Checkbox · Radio · Switch · Toggle · Slider

All three selection controls separate the hit target from the drawn box: the target is one step up the space scale, so a 20px box gets a 24px target with no visual padding. The box border is 1.5px — the medium width — which is what gives these controls their weight against a dark surface.

Chip · Badge · Tag

Three separate objects that are easy to collapse into one and shouldn't be. Chip is interactive and selectable. Badge is promotional and skewed. Tag is passive metadata.

Chip Confirmed

Fully round, 1.5px border, transparent until selected. Hover moves the border to lime-900 — a very dark lime, almost black-green — rather than lightening it, which keeps hover quiet across a long filter row.

Badge Confirmed

The system's signature marker, and the one component most likely to be redrawn wrongly: Space Grotesk Bold, uppercase, 12px, 2px radius, 14px tall — and skewed -10.89deg. The skew is on the surface only; the text is counter-skewed back to upright. Do not round it, do not un-skew it, do not set it in Inter.

Parameter panel · Tool bar · Presets

The densest surfaces in the product, and the ones the public site never shows. These come from the 557-screen capture set: the editor's right-hand rail, the tool bar that floats over the work surface, and the preset grid that appears in every studio.

Confirmed from capture set

Floating tool bar

A pill container of 48px pill tools. The container sits on the card surface (#1c1e20, sampled); the active tool is one step lighter at 10% white, and may carry a badge. It scrolls horizontally rather than wrapping.

Parameter panel

Color Grading

The correction this surface forced. I had built the switch with a lime checked track, because lime is the brand colour and lime is what every CTA uses. Sampling the pixels here returned #01c317 — the success green. Higgsfield reserves lime for actions you take and uses green for state that is on. That distinction is not visible anywhere on the public site.

Other details worth keeping: the panel surface is #131517 (page-primary), one step darker than the cards it floats over — the opposite of the usual convention. Parameter rows are 56px with 8%-white dividers. The footer pins three controls at 48px and lets only the primary grow. And the primary carries the lime gloss gradient, which I had assumed was marketing-only: sampling its top edge returned #eefd17.

Preset grid — selected state

Selection is a 2px lime ring and a lime circular check centred on the thumbnail — both, not either. Worth copying exactly: the ring alone is too quiet at this thumbnail size, which is presumably why they doubled it.

Validation

Checks run against the live product, and the four things that came back wrong on the first pass. Honest accounting is the point of the exercise.

Fixed after comparison

  • Badge skewDrawn as a plain rounded pill first. The product skews the surface -10.89deg and counter-skews the text. Corrected.
  • Grey rampStarted on a true neutral grey. The product's UI ramp (cool) is blue-leaning at every step. Replaced.
  • Focus ringBuilt as a single outline. The product draws a 2px page-coloured gap then a 4px brand ring, so the ring floats clear of the control. Rebuilt.
  • Field borderGiven a 1px border. The product uses no border at all — depth is a 5% fill, and focus is an inset ring. Removed.
  • Switch was limeBuilt with a lime checked track, since lime is the brand and every CTA uses it. Pixel-sampling the shipped editor returned #01c317 — the success green. Lime is for actions you take; green is for state that is on. Corrected.
  • Gloss thought to be marketing-onlyThe lime gradient was applied only to hero CTAs. Sampling the editor's primary returned #eefd17 at its top edge — the same gloss. Now available in-app via data-gloss.
  • Panel surface assumed lighterFloating panels were given the card surface. They are #131517 — one step darker than the cards they float over. Corrected.

Known gaps

  • Light themeStill derived. The capture set contains no light-mode product UI — the one light screen in it is generated artwork, not chrome. Higgsfield ships light-mode hooks but no full light semantic block.
  • button-lg geometryOnly its icon size ships as a rule. Height and radius continue the ladder and are cross-checked against the app-scale button at the same height.
  • List & paginationStill assembled from confirmed row primitives. The table itself is now confirmed — see Account.
  • Icon glyphsSpec confirmed, drawings original. Higgsfield's icon set and logomark are not reproduced.
