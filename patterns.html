Scoring

spyy is a judgement product: nearly every screen tells the user how sure it is about something. The reference product solves the same problem in its virality predictor, and the way it does it is worth copying precisely — because the obvious approach is worse.

Confirmed — capture #500 Serves P3 attribution · P4 tiering
This replaces the six coloured score cards in the current mockup.

The mockup gives each score its own bar colour — green, blue, purple, amber, green, red. The reference scoring surface makes every bar the same lime and puts the qualitative reading on one calibrated gradient scale instead. On six-plus dimensions that matters: with per-bar colour, a red “Brand Safety 38” and an amber “Conversion Intent 42” read as severity, when the data means different axes. Low brand safety is a warning; low conversion intent is just a fact about the creative. One bar colour keeps them distinct.

Confidence & verdicts

The brief is unusually explicit that resolution and attribution carry a confidence score and that a human must be able to act on it. So confidence needs a shape that reads at a glance and never pretends to be certainty.

Confirmed — captures #125–126 Serves P1 resolution · P3 attribution

Score gauge

A thick ring whose arc colour is driven by the verdict, not the brand — lime when good, amber when marginal, red when bad — with a matching pill under the value. The good state also glows, which is what makes a pass feel like a pass. This is the reference moodboard quality ring; spyy uses it for resolution confidence and adaptability.

Async scan

“A scan is an async job: resolve, fan out to adapters, persist, tier the analysis, report partial results as they land. It must survive one source failing and report which sources were covered.” That sentence rules out a page spinner. Results arrive unevenly and the page has to stay useful the whole time.

Confirmed — capture #501 Serves P2 coverage · P6 orchestration

Processing frame

A 2px tint outline around the region that is working, with a status pill notched into its top-left corner. The reference product uses it to say “something is happening here” without blocking the rest of the page — exactly what partial results need. The frame changes colour on completion and failure rather than disappearing, so the region keeps its history.

Candidate picker P1

Which one is the brand?

Three identities matched “nordic labs”. Pick one — the scan runs against it.

Why it looks like this

  • Radio, not a dropdownExactly one is correct and the choice is consequential. A closed select would hide the alternatives and the confidence spread.
  • Confidence as a number, right-alignedSo three candidates can be compared down a column. Below 60 it goes amber — a weak top match should look weak.
  • Source badges on every rowThe same brand name resolves to a Meta page, a TikTok account and an app listing. Which platform a candidate is on is half the decision.
  • “None of these” is always presentThe brief says resolution will sometimes be wrong. The escape hatch is part of the pattern, not an edge case.

Rules the patterns share

Colour discipline

  • Tint means “this is the thing”Selected, filled, active, on. Never severity.
  • Status colour means severityOnly on verdicts, coverage status, impact chips and a value that is genuinely a warning. Never on a bar.
  • Confidence is never a colourIt is a separate line in the tertiary colour, because confidence and value answer different questions.
  • One calibration per screenIf two score sets appear together they share the gradient scale; two scales on one screen means neither is readable.

Interaction

  • Focus is always the component focus ring2px page-coloured gap + 4px tint, or an inset 1.5px ring inside a field. Patterns never invent their own.
  • Hover on a transparent surface is 5% whiteRows, cards, candidates and option cards all use the one overlay.
  • Selection doubles up when the target is smallPreset tiles and preset-sized targets take a ring and a mark; a chip or an option card takes a border and a colour change.
  • Pending is a sweep, not a spinnerInside a row or track. Spinners are for whole regions, in the processing frame’s badge.
